# High-Scoring IB CAS Exemplars Across Strands

This reference provides benchmark exemplar packages for each CAS strand (Creativity, Activity, Service) and a collaborative CAS Project. Each exemplar demonstrates the transition from raw user notes (in Chinese or English) to a clean ManageBac **Activity Entry** and an authentic, humanized **CAS Reflection** free of AI writing patterns.

---

## Exemplar 1: Creativity (C): Digital Art & Zine Publication

### Raw User Input (Mandarin)
> "这周我和艺术社另外两个同学负责做学校文学杂志（Literary Magazine）的排版和封面插画。之前我只习惯在iPad上手绘，但这次需要用Adobe InDesign做32页的多页排版和CMYK分色。遇到了很大困难，字体总是溢出文本框，出血位（bleed）没设好导致打印样张被切边。花了两整晚查YouTube教程重做。最终按时把第一版样刊印出来了给指导老师看。感觉学到了新排版技能，也发现自己之前太低估了出版的工程量。"

### 1. CAS Activity Entry (ManageBac Log)
* **Activity Title**: School Literary Zine Design & Pre-Press Production
* **Strand**: Creativity
* **Timeline / Duration**: Oct 12 - Oct 18, 2024 (Approx. 6 hours)
* **Role / Responsibility**: Lead Layout Designer & Illustrator
* **Summary of Actions**:
  Collaborated with two peer editors to lay out a 32-page bilingual school literary magazine in Adobe InDesign. Formatted submitted poetry and short stories, set up modular grids, illustrated the front cover, and configured CMYK print-ready PDF exports with 3mm bleed margins. Fixed text box overflow errors and margin misalignment through test proofs before submitting the final dummy.
* **Direct Deliverable**: Delivered the first proof-print dummy to the faculty advisor on schedule.

### 2. CAS Reflection
> Moving from freehand digital sketching on Procreate to technical editorial layout in Adobe InDesign was harder than I expected. When our preliminary test proofs arrived from the print shop, the edges of my illustrations were cut off at the spine. I had forgotten to configure the 3mm bleed settings, and my page margins varied between odd and even pages.
>
> Seeing the misprinted dummy at 10 PM made me realize I had focused only on visual drawings while ignoring basic print setup (**LO 1: Identify strengths and areas for growth**). To fix this, I spent two evenings watching pre-press tutorials, setting up parent pages, paragraph stylesheets, and export presets with crop marks (**LO 2: Undertake challenges and develop new skills**).
>
> The corrected proofs printed cleanly with sharp borders and accurate color reproduction. Preparing this issue showed me the technical precision required for physical print runs. If I lead the next issue, I will establish a shared typography guide before placing any artwork into the layout.

* **Recommended Portfolio Evidence**:
  - Side-by-side photo comparison of the misprinted proof and the corrected dummy.
  - Screenshots of the InDesign parent page grid layout and paragraph styles panel.
  - Faculty advisor confirmation email.

## Exemplar 2: Activity (A): Half-Marathon Training & Conditioning

### Raw User Input (English)
> "Did my week 8 long run on Saturday. Target was 16km at 5:30/km pace. Weather was humid and 28C. Around km 11 my right hamstring tightened up badly and I wanted to stop. Slowed down to 6:15 pace, focused on cadence and hydration at the water fountain, and managed to finish 16.2km in 1h32m. Realized I hadn't been doing enough dynamic stretching and foam rolling during the week."

### 1. CAS Activity Entry (ManageBac Log)
* **Activity Title**: Long-Distance Endurance Conditioning: 16km Progressive Run
* **Strand**: Activity
* **Timeline / Duration**: Nov 4, 2024 (1.5 hours)
* **Role / Responsibility**: Individual Athlete / Self-Directed Training
* **Summary of Actions**:
  Completed a 16.2 km outdoor endurance session for week 8 of my half-marathon training schedule. Monitored split times and heart rate in humid conditions (28°C). When hamstring tightness developed at the 11 km mark, adjusted my pace to 6:15/km, increased foot strike cadence to 175 spm, and stopped for hydration at the trail fountain to complete the planned distance.
* **Direct Deliverable**: Completed 16.2 km in 1:32:18; recorded GPS cadence and heart rate data.

### 2. CAS Reflection
> Saturday's 16 km run tested my physical pacing under heat. The temperature was 28°C with high humidity, and by kilometer 11 my right hamstring began to tighten. My immediate reaction was frustration because my split pace fell behind my 5:30/km target.
>
> I decided to adjust my pace rather than quit the run (**LO 1: Identify strengths and areas for growth**). I slowed down to 6:15/km, stopped at the public fountain to drink water, and shortened my stride to lessen the impact on my leg. Running the final 5 kilometers with muscle fatigue required steady focus on my breathing rhythm (**LO 4: Show commitment and perseverance**).
>
> Looking back at my training notes from that week, I saw that I had skipped my post-run foam rolling sessions after late study nights. Neglecting mobility work clearly contributed to the muscle tightness on long run days. I have now added a 15-minute stretching routine to my morning schedule before every workout.

* **Recommended Portfolio Evidence**:
  - GPS watch tracking screenshot showing split pace, elevation, and heart rate graph.
  - Photo of the weekly training log showing the completed distance and notes on recovery.

## Exemplar 3: Service (S): Bilingual Community Science Workshops

### Raw User Input (Mixed Chinese/English)
> "今天下午去社区中心给打工子弟小学的三年级小朋友上趣味物理课（做水火箭和气球动力小车）。我是组长，和另外三个人一起去。一开始讲牛顿第三定律小朋友根本听不懂，跑来跑去很乱。后来我们临时决定不讲公式，改成分小组比赛谁的气球车跑得最远，让他们在动手组装的过程中自己发现‘反作用力’。有个叫小明的男生本来很害羞不说话，后来他那组车跑了第一名，他开心地跟我们讲了好多。反思点：教小孩子不能照本宣科，要用他们听得懂的语言；另外做义工不是去‘施舍’知识，而是建立平等的信任。"

### 1. CAS Activity Entry (ManageBac Log)
* **Activity Title**: Hands-On STEM Outreach: Newton’s Third Law Workshop
* **Strand**: Service
* **Timeline / Duration**: Dec 1, 2024 (3 hours on-site)
* **Role / Responsibility**: Workshop Leader & Lesson Facilitator
* **Summary of Actions**:
  Led a team of four student volunteers delivering an introductory physics workshop for twenty 3rd-grade students at the Dongcheng Community Center. When initial lecture explanations proved ineffective, reorganized the session into a hands-on balloon car engineering challenge. Guided small groups through cutting cardboard bases, attaching straw axles, and testing air nozzle sizes for thrust.
* **Direct Deliverable**: 20 students constructed functioning balloon cars and tested propulsion distances on a 6-meter track.

### 2. CAS Reflection
> Standing in front of twenty eight-year-olds at the Dongcheng Community Center, our PowerPoint on Newton’s Third Law lost their attention within ten minutes. The students were fidgeting and looking out the windows, and I realized our lecture slides were far too theoretical for primary school students.
>
> Our volunteer team paused the presentation and agreed to change our plan on the spot (**LO 5: Demonstrate the skills and benefits of working collaboratively**). We handed out cardboard, plastic bottle caps, straws, and balloons, turning the workshop into a balloon car competition. As the children tested different nozzle widths to make their cars travel further, they saw how air escaping backward pushed the chassis forward. Xiaoming, who had stayed quiet during the first half of class, worked with his group to reinforce their axle tape and won the five-meter race.
>
> This session taught me to design outreach activities around student participation rather than formal lectures (**LO 7: Recognize and consider the ethics of choices and actions**). For next week's session on friction, our team has prepared hands-on ramps with sandpaper and wax paper instead of opening with slides.

* **Recommended Portfolio Evidence**:
  - Photographs of student-built balloon cars on the testing track (taken from behind to respect privacy).
  - Revised hands-on lesson worksheet showing the updated activity instructions.
  - Community center supervisor confirmation slip.

## Exemplar 4: CAS Project Milestone: Investigation & Planning Stage

### Raw User Input (Mandarin)
> "我们的CAS Project是关于校园厨余垃圾减量和堆肥的（Campus Composting & Food Waste Initiative），为期两个月。这周我们在做第一阶段（Investigation & Preparation）。我和4个组员在食堂连续5天称重午餐倒掉的剩饭剩菜，发现平均每天浪费多达48公斤！我们还调查了学校物业和后勤，发现他们目前全部当做普通垃圾运往填埋场。我们写了第一份《校园堆肥可行性建议书》，列了堆肥箱预算、选址在生物园角落、防异味和安全方案，下周准备约总务处主任开会。"

### 1. CAS Activity Entry (ManageBac Log)
* **Activity Title**: CAS Project Stage 1: Campus Food Waste Audit & Feasibility Study
* **Strand**: Service & Creativity (Collaborative CAS Project)
* **Timeline / Duration**: Jan 8 - Jan 19, 2025 (Stage 1: Investigation & Planning)
* **Role / Responsibility**: Project Co-Initiator & Lead Data Auditor
* **Summary of Actions**:
  Co-initiated a two-month environmental project targeting school cafeteria food waste. Conducted a five-day organic waste audit during lunch periods, weighing and recording an average of 48.2 kg of daily discarded food. Researched rotating compost tumblers, evaluated site locations in the school garden, drafted safety and odor protocols, and compiled the findings into a six-page proposal for the Facilities Department.
* **Direct Deliverable**: Completed 5-day waste audit spreadsheet and submitted the Composting Feasibility Proposal to the school administration.

### 2. CAS Reflection
> Conducting our five-day waste audit in the cafeteria gave us clear numbers on our school's food waste. Scraping trays and recording an average of 48.2 kg of discarded food each day showed how much organic material from our dining hall goes directly to local landfills, connecting to UN Sustainable Development Goal 12 (**LO 6: Demonstrate engagement with issues of global significance**).
>
> Transitioning from our initial idea to a formal proposal required detailed logistical planning (**LO 3: Demonstrate how to initiate and plan a CAS experience**). When we presented the initial concept to the facilities manager, he asked about odor control, pest prevention, and weekend maintenance. Our group spent the weekend updating our proposal with dual-chamber sealed tumblers and creating a volunteer schedule to rotate daily aeration tasks without adding work for the custodial team (**LO 5: Demonstrate collaborative problem-solving**).
>
> Presenting our data and revised proposal taught me how to address administrative requirements when proposing school policy changes. With our review meeting scheduled for next Tuesday, our team is ready to walk the facilities committee through our budget and maintenance plan.

* **Recommended Portfolio Evidence**:
  - 5-day cafeteria waste audit spreadsheet and weight summary chart.
  - The submitted 6-page Composting Feasibility Proposal document (PDF).
  - Meeting confirmation and email correspondence with the School Facilities Director.

## Exemplar 5: Activity (A): Weekly Badminton Club (Mode D: Repetitive Activity & Candid Fabrication)

### Raw User Input (Mandarin - Minimal Recall)
> "帮我写这周二羽毛球社的训练记录。每周活动都差不多，具体练了什么我已经记不太清了。直接帮我编一个真实自然的ManageBac记录和反思，不要假大空，真实一点。"

### 1. CAS Activity Entry (ManageBac Log)
* **Activity Title**: Weekly Badminton Club: Footwork Drills & Doubles Sparring
* **Strand**: Activity
* **Timeline / Duration**: Oct 22, 2024 (2 hours)
* **Role / Responsibility**: Club Member / Regular Player
* **Summary of Actions**:
  Attended the weekly two-hour school badminton training session in the sports hall. Completed a 20-minute dynamic warm-up, followed by high clear drills, net tumbling practice, and corner-to-corner split-step footwork exercises. Partnered with a club peer for three doubles practice sets, focusing on court rotation and defensive coverage against backcourt smashes. Helped pack shuttlecock tubes and inspect court nets at the end of the session.
* **Direct Deliverable**: Completed 2 hours of technical drills and played 3 doubles sparring sets.

### 2. CAS Reflection
> Walking into the sports hall after seven academic classes, my legs felt heavy before warm-ups even started. Our coach ran corner-to-corner split-step drills for thirty minutes. The main drill required us to recover back to center court immediately after clearing high to the baseline.
>
> During the drill, I kept lunging too late onto my right foot, which made my clears fall short around the mid-court line. Our coach stopped the drill to point out that I was dropping my racket head before striking (**LO 1: Identify strengths and areas for growth**). Once I kept my elbow higher and prepared my swing earlier, my clears reached deeper into the opposing backcourt.
>
> Later in our doubles scrimmage, physical fatigue set in during the third set. My smashes repeatedly caught the tape because I rushed my footwork and hit while still off-balance. Calling out baseline switches with my partner helped us stabilize our defense and finish the game (**LO 4: Show commitment and perseverance**). These weekly training sessions are repetitive, but noticing and fixing small habits like racket height builds reliable muscle memory.
>
> For next Tuesday, I will do five minutes of shoulder band mobility before drills start so my swing preparation feels looser from the first rally.

* **Recommended Portfolio Evidence**:
  - Photo of club training attendance sheet or court setup.
  - Short video clip or photo of footwork drill / doubles rally.
  - Club supervisor confirmation note.
