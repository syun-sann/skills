# NUS Application Essay Revision Skill (`nus-essay-revision`)

This skill is only for small tweaks of your essays regarding language style and task achievement tailored specifically for **National University of Singapore (NUS)** undergraduate short response questions.

Use cautiously as using AI for generating NUS essays is risky, you should always draft on your own before using any AI tools.

---

## 🎯 What This Skill Does

The `nus-essay-revision` skill enforces the strict standards set by the NUS Office of Admissions:
- **Standard British English**: Enforces UK spelling (*realise*, *analyse*, *programme*, *specialisation*, *rigour*) and Commonwealth academic vocabulary (*at university*, *modules*, *read Law*).
- **Official Character Reply Limits**: Tailored for the official **600-character cap** (Questions 1–3) and **1,100-character cap** (Question 4).
- **NUS Core Values Integration**: Seamlessly guides and embeds the five NUS values (**Innovation, Resilience, Excellence, Respect, Integrity**) in Question 4.
- **Simplicity, Directness & Zero Fluff**: Cuts throat-clearing openings, wordy connectors, and cliché AI buzzwords.
- **Voice Preservation**: Elevates clarity and impact through minimal-invasive editing without erasing the applicant's authentic voice.

## 📋 Official NUS Short Questions & Limits Reference

1. **Question 1 (Preparation Outside Curriculum)**:
   *Tell us something you have done outside your school curriculum to prepare yourself for your chosen degree programme(s).*  
   **Limit: Max 600 characters with spaces** (~80–95 words).

2. **Question 2 (Resilience & Learning from Failure)**:
   *Describe an instance when you did not succeed in accomplishing something on your first attempt but succeeded on subsequent attempts. How and what did you learn from your initial failure, and what changes did you make to your approach to eventually succeed?*  
   **Limit: Max 600 characters with spaces** (~80–95 words).

3. **Question 3 (Meaningful Experience & Concrete Impact)**:
   *Share something that is meaningful to you and explain how it has impacted you in a concrete way.*  
   **Limit: Max 600 characters with spaces** (~80–95 words).

4. **Question 4 (Proudest Achievement & NUS Values)**:
   *What is your proudest achievement, and how did you accomplish it with the help or inspiration from others? Please also explain how it exemplifies some of the five NUS values of Innovation, Resilience, Excellence, Respect and Integrity.*  
   **Limit: Max 1,100 characters with spaces** (~150–175 words).
