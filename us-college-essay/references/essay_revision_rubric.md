# US College Admissions Essay Revision & Rubric Guide

This guide defines the diagnostic standards, editing principles, anti-hallucination rules, and anti-cliché filters used when reviewing, revising, and elevating US college application essays (Common App, UC PIQs, and supplementals).

---

## 1. Factual Integrity & Zero-Fabrication Mandate (绝对保真原则)

### The Core Rule
Admissions essays represent the student's authentic lived experience and ethical character. **Under no circumstance may the consultant or model invent, fabricate, exaggerate, or assume facts, metrics, awards, roles, or personal anecdotes not present in the student's draft or notes.**

### Operational Rules for Missing Information
1. **Never Invent Data:** If the student writes *"we raised money for the shelter"*, DO NOT arbitrarily write *"we raised $3,500 for the city dog shelter"*.
2. **Flag and Inquire:** In Part 1 of the output (*修改状态与追问清单*), explicitly list every question needed to fill narrative or quantitative voids.
3. **Use Explicit Placeholders in Polished Text:** If a missing detail is required to maintain grammatical and logical flow, use bracketed placeholders such as `[具体金额/参与人数需确认]` or `[插上某种实验仪器的具体名称]`, ensuring the student knows exactly what to substitute.

### Reviewer Proposals vs. Student Reality (批注启发性例子的甄别与核验)
批注者在指出问题后，经常会给出建议性举例（例如：“这里缺少细节，比如你可以写你调试电路时被电烙铁烫到……”）。
- **本质属性**：此类“例如”是批注者为了具象化修改方向而提出的**启发性提案（Proposal）**，绝不等于学生的真实档案！
- **处理红线与交互机制**：
  1. **严禁直接采用**：严禁直接将顾问/批注者脑暴出的假设性例子当成既定事实写进新稿。
  2. **类似 `/grill-me` 的先问后写互动机制**：在生成终稿前，必须主动向用户提问对齐，不得草率代填。
  3. **提供充足的决策 Context**：向用户追问时，必须讲清楚这个例子在上下文中的**具体功能**（例如：“此处的例子是为了证明你的自主探究深度”、“弥补情感转折突兀的缺口”），以便用户准确判断。
  4. **双重追问与替换引导**：
     - **追问一（核实真实性）**：学生是否真有此经历？
     - **追问二（探索替代项）**：若无此经历，引导学生提供能够达到同等证明力和叙事效果的真实经历/细节。
  5. **终稿占位防护**：若因特殊情况用户未即时回复，新稿中必须使用醒目占位符 `[顾问提案示例，需学生确认真实性或提供真实经历替换：...]`。

## 2. Preserving the Authentic Student Voice (守护原生特质)

Admissions officers read thousands of essays each cycle. The biggest giveaway of an artificial or over-edited essay is a homogenized, corporate, or overly academic voice that no 17-year-old would ever use.

* **Target Voice:** An articulate, reflective, intellectually curious, and self-aware high school senior.
* **Tone:** Sincere, thoughtful, proactive, and grounded. Neither self-aggrandizing nor falsely modest.
* **Preserve Quirks & Authentic Perspective:** Keep the student's unique metaphors, personal idioms, niche interests, and emotional turning points intact. Polish the execution without altering the student's worldview.
* **Minimal-Invasive Surgery on Strong Sections:** If a paragraph already communicates voice and vulnerability effectively, do not rewrite it from scratch. Focus surgery on weak transitions, vague claims, or structural disconnects.

## 3. Anti-Slop & Humanizer Filter (深度融合 Humanizer 与 Stop-Slop)

Essays must eliminate robotic, predictable AI patterns while preserving intellectual depth, semantic accuracy, and the applicant's authentic lived experience.

### A. Prohibited Vocabulary & Inflated Claims (拒绝假大空)
| Category | Prohibited Words / Phrases | Preferred Alternatives / Fixes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pompous Nouns** | *tapestry, beacon, testament, realm, catalyst, plethora, symphony, kaleidoscope, cornerstone* | Replace with concrete nouns or straightforward framing. Let facts prove importance. |
| **Overused Adjectives** | *multifaceted, profound, indelible, paramount, invaluable, transformative, pivotal, vital* | Remove the empty modifier; describe the tangible action or consequence. |
| **Vague Action Verbs** | *delve into, foster, navigate the complexities of, embark upon, weave, underscore* | Use direct, active verbs (*built, debugged, negotiated, calculated, revised, tested*). |
| **Throat-Clearing Openings** | *"From a young age...", "Since time immemorial...", "Have you ever wondered...", "It is worth noting that..."* | Cut immediately; start in media res or with a direct factual observation. |
| **Formulaic Conclusions** | *"In conclusion...", "This experience taught me...", "Looking toward the future...", "paving the way for..."* | End on a forward-looking resonance, reflective counterfactual, or open synthesis. |

### B. Structural AI Tells to Eliminate (打破典型 AI 句式套路)
1. **Trailing `-ing` Shallow Analysis**:
   - **AI Pattern**: Ending sentences with lazy participial phrases (*"...allowing me to embrace my potential, highlighting the importance of resilience and shaping my worldview."*).
   - **Fix**: Cut the trailing tail. If the reflection matters, state it in its own concrete sentence with real causal mechanism.
2. **Binary Contrasts & Negative Listings**:
   - **AI Pattern**: *"It was not merely about writing code, but about forging human connection."*
   - **Fix**: State the positive truth directly: *"I realized the software was useless if the elderly residents couldn't read the buttons."*
3. **Rule-of-Three Overkill**:
   - **AI Pattern**: Forcing three parallel adjectives, nouns, or verbs into every rhythmic beat (*"...with passion, dedication, and unwavering curiosity."*).
   - **Fix**: Use two precise items, or a single strong, accurate noun.
4. **Synthetic Pull-Quotes & Slogans**:
   - **AI Pattern**: Crafting polished PR slogans (*"In the silence of the laboratory, I found the music of science."*).
   - **Fix**: Ground in sensory, imperfect reality (*"My pipetting thumb cramped after the twentieth dilution, but the agar plate finally stayed clear."*).

### C. The 5-Point Quick Check (交付前快速自检)
1. **Agency Check**: Does an actual human being do the action in every sentence? (No *"the project enlightened me"* $\to$ *"I learned to calibrate the sensor"*).
2. **Inflated Significance Check**: Are ordinary actions treated like historic milestones? (Cut *"marked a pivotal turning point in my trajectory"*).
3. **Trailing Fluff Check**: Are there any loose `-ing` phrases dragging at sentence ends? (Delete or convert to direct assertions).
4. **Cadence & Rhythm Check**: Do 3 consecutive sentences share identical length? (Break one or combine).
5. **Authentic Tone Check**: Does this sound like a real, articulate 17-year-old in conversation, or a PR marketing brochure?

## 4. Show, Don't Tell: Micro-Details & Operational Grounding

Admissions officers remember tangible scenes and specific operations, not broad thematic summaries.

* **Weak (Generic Summary):**
  > *"I faced many logistical hurdles while organizing the regional debate tournament, which honed my leadership skills."*
* **Strong (Operational Grounding):**
  > *"When the school WiFi collapsed twenty minutes before Round 1, I sprinted between five classrooms manually distributing paper pairings and reassigning timer duties to spare volunteers."*

---

## 5. Rubric: Admissions Officer Scoring Lens

When evaluating and revising essays, evaluate along 5 dimensions:

1. **Thesis & Core Narrative Arc (核心主线与叙事张力):**
   * Is there a clear emotional or intellectual throughline?
   * Does the essay move from challenge/curiosity $\to$ action $\to$ realization/growth?
2. **Agency & Proactivity (主体性与行动力):**
   * Does the applicant take active ownership of decisions, or are they a passive observer of events happening around them?
   * Are actions driven by internal conviction rather than external compulsion?
3. **Intellectual Vitality & Depth (思辨深度与反思能力):**
   * Does the reflection demonstrate nuance, self-critique, or insight into why things happened?
   * Does the student connect personal experiences to broader intellectual questions or community dynamics?
4. **Voice & Tone Calibration (语言质感与语调):**
   * Is the tone mature yet authentically young?
   * Are sentence rhythms varied (mixing short punchy sentences with compound-complex sentences)?
5. **Word Economy & Prompt Fit (篇幅经济度与题目契合度):**
   * Does every sentence pull its weight?
   * Does the essay strictly abide by the prompt's word ceilings (Common App $\le 650$ words; UC PIQ $\le 350$ words; supplementals as specified)?
