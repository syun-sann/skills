# Common App & UC PIQ Admissions Guide (Primary Source Reference)

This reference guide provides official criteria, verbatim prompts, word limits, and stylistic guidelines for the **Common Application Personal Statement** and the **University of California (UC) Personal Insight Questions (PIQs)**.

---

## Part 1: Common App Personal Statement

### 1. Primary Sources & System Specifications
* **Official Prompt Directory:** [https://www.commonapp.org/apply/essay-prompts](https://www.commonapp.org/apply/essay-prompts)
* **Application Guide:** [https://www.commonapp.org/plan/how-to-apply](https://www.commonapp.org/plan/how-to-apply)
* **Strict Word Limit:** **250 words minimum to 650 words maximum**.
  * The system live counter validates word count. Below 250 words blocks submission; above 650 words truncates/blocks text.
  * **Strategic Sweet Spot:** **550–645 words**. Allows full narrative arc and reflective depth without risk of truncation.

### 2. Verbatim Official 7 Prompts
1. **Prompt 1 (Background / Identity / Talent):**
   > *"Some students have a background, identity, interest, or talent that is so meaningful they believe their application would be incomplete without it. If this sounds like you, then please share your story."*
2. **Prompt 2 (Obstacles / Setbacks / Failure):**
   > *"The lessons we take from obstacles we encounter can be fundamental to later success. Recount a time when you faced a challenge, setback, or failure. How did it affect you, and what did you learn from the experience?"*
3. **Prompt 3 (Challenging a Belief or Idea):**
   > *"Reflect on a time when you questioned or challenged a belief or idea. What prompted your thinking? What was the outcome?"*
4. **Prompt 4 (Gratitude):**
   > *"Reflect on something that someone has done for you that has made you happy or thankful in a surprising way. How has this gratitude affected or motivated you?"*
5. **Prompt 5 (Personal Growth / Realization):**
   > *"Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization that sparked a period of personal growth and a new understanding of yourself or others."*
6. **Prompt 6 (Engaging Topic / Intellectual Curiosity):**
   > *"Describe a topic, idea, or concept you find so engaging that it makes you lose all track of time. Why does it captivate you? What or who do you turn to when you want to learn more?"*
7. **Prompt 7 (Topic of Choice):**
   > *"Share an essay on any topic of your choice. It can be one you've already written, one that responds to a different prompt, or one of your own design."*

### 3. Core Stylistic Expectations for Common App
* **Narrative Arc & Show-Don't-Tell:** Engaging hook, organic development, psychological insight, vulnerability, and personal growth.
* **Authentic High-School Voice:** Reflective and insightful, avoiding pseudo-intellectual jargon or flat resume listing.
* **Platform Constraints:** No tabs or paragraph indentations; single empty line between paragraphs. Review print preview for smart quotes and dashes.

## Part 2: UC Personal Insight Questions (PIQs)

### 1. Primary Sources & System Specifications
* **Official Freshman PIQ Portal:** [https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-freshman/personal-insight-questions.html](https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-freshman/personal-insight-questions.html)
* **Counselor Guide to PIQs:** [https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/counselors/preparing-freshman-students/personal-insight-questions.html](https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/counselors/preparing-freshman-students/personal-insight-questions.html)
* **Format:** Freshman applicants must choose and answer **exactly 4 out of the 8 prompts**.
* **Strict Word Limit:** **Maximum 350 words per prompt** (all 8 prompts carry equal weight; recommended length: 300–350 words).

### 2. Verbatim Official 8 Freshman Prompts
1. **Prompt 1 (Leadership Experience):**
   > *"Describe an example of your leadership experience in which you have positively influenced others, helped resolve disputes or contributed to group efforts over time."*
2. **Prompt 2 (Creative Side):**
   > *"Every person has a creative side, and it can be expressed in many ways: problem solving, original and innovative thinking, and artistically, to name just a few. Describe how you express your creative side."*
3. **Prompt 3 (Greatest Talent or Skill):**
   > *"What would you say is your greatest talent or skill? How have you developed and demonstrated that talent over time?"*
4. **Prompt 4 (Educational Opportunity or Barrier):**
   > *"Describe how you have taken advantage of a significant educational opportunity or worked to overcome an educational barrier you have faced."*
5. **Prompt 5 (Significant Challenge):**
   > *"Describe the most significant challenge you have faced and the steps you have taken to overcome this challenge. How has this challenge affected your academic achievement?"*
6. **Prompt 6 (Academic Subject):**
   > *"Think about an academic subject that inspires you. Describe how you have furthered this interest inside and/or outside of the classroom."*
7. **Prompt 7 (School or Community):**
   > *"What have you done to make your school or your community a better place?"*
8. **Prompt 8 (Stand Out / Open Prompt):**
   > *"Beyond what has already been shared in your application, what do you believe makes you stand out as a strong candidate for admission to the University of California?"*

### 3. Critical Stylistic Distinctions: UC PIQ vs. Common App
* **"An Interview on Paper":** UC does not interview applicants. Evaluators spend 6–8 minutes on a complete application. They want clarity, facts, and impact.
* **Tell, Then Show (Direct & Factual):**
  * **DO NOT** use dramatic hooks, scene-setting, metaphorical fiction, or waking up to alarm clocks.
  * **DO NOT** quote famous figures (waste of word count; evaluators want the applicant's voice).
  * **DO NOT** spend words describing other people (mentors, parents). Ensure $\ge 80\%$ focuses directly on the applicant's actions.
* **The CAR Framework:**
  1. **Context:** Problem, constraint, background, or intellectual premise.
  2. **Action:** Specific individual actions using first-person active verbs (`I spearheaded`, `I analyzed`, `I rebuilt`). Avoid hiding behind "we".
  3. **Result & Impact:** Measurable quantitative/qualitative outcome and self-reflection on what was learned.

## Part 3: Comparative Matrix

| Dimension | Common App Personal Statement | UC Personal Insight Questions (PIQs) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quantity** | 1 essay | 4 essays (selected from 8 options) |
| **Word Limit** | 250 – 650 words | Up to 350 words per prompt |
| **Primary Genre** | Narrative & introspective reflection | Informational "interview on paper" |
| **Pacing / Hooks** | Literary hook, scene immersion, pacing | Immediate direct answer; no hook gimmicks |
| **Narrative Architecture**| Conflict $\to$ Epiphany $\to$ Transformation | CAR: Context $\to$ Action $\to$ Result/Impact |
| **Quotes & Dialogue**| Permissible when enhancing character arc | Strictly discouraged; wastes word limit |
| **Core Evaluator Question**| *"Who is this person and how do they think?"* | *"What did this student specifically do and achieve?"* |
